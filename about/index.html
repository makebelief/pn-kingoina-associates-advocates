About the firm

Prepared with care.
Focused on the client.

P. N. King’oina & Associates Advocates is a Kenyan legal practice providing considered representation, legal advice and document preparation across a focused range of matters.

Our approach

Legal service that starts with understanding the issue.

Every matter is different. The firm’s approach is to understand the facts, identify the legal position and give the client a clear view of the available next steps.

That means practical communication, careful preparation and representation shaped around the needs of the particular instruction rather than a one-size-fits-all process.

Precision in Practice.

The firm’s brand promise is simple: details matter. Whether the instruction concerns a dispute, a criminal matter, a family issue, a property transaction, a commercial relationship or the workplace, the goal is to bring structure and direction to the legal problem.
